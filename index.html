WP6 AI Operational Role by Experimental Arm

The design principle relies on instructional support differences, not the tool. Arms A and B use the same masked model on the same platform to avoid confounding the training effect.

  • Arm A (Experimental) - Supported AI Use: Curricular tasks paired with explicit AI literacy instruction and guided prompting scaffolds.
  • Arm B (Active Control) - Unsupported AI Use: AI use without instruction. Same AI as Arm A, but no prompt coaching or literacy guidance.
  • Arm C (Baseline Control) - No AI in Class: Identical curricular learning goals taught via traditional textbooks, inquiry sheets, and teacher instruction (Zero AI Integration). Control arms B and C will receive the instructional materials after the study.

The 3-Session Intervention Sequence

Session format: ~3 hours per session, 3–5 weeks apart. Each session opens with a pre-test and closes with a post-test.

  • Session 1: Prompt Engineering. Foundational interaction, goal-oriented prompt structuring, and iterative dialogue in STEM tasks.
  • Session 2: Critical AI Literacy. Identifying flawed outputs, misconceptions and bias; reflecting on the accuracy and ethical implications of AI outputs.
  • Session 3: Authentic Inquiry. Collaborative problem solving on real-life challenges under uncertainty with evidence synthesis. (Includes a science literacy assessment).

Pedagogical Framework

  • Inquiry-Based Learning (IBL): Students formulate scientific hypotheses, analyze phenomena, and construct evidence-based arguments rather than passively receiving answers.
  • Real-Life Socio-Scientific Relevance: Tasks anchor in authentic challenges (climate, plastic waste, energy) to enhance girls' and underrepresented learners' engagement.
  • Cognitive Load & Epistemic Trust: Balancing the benefits of automated support with critical evaluation to foster metacognitive self-regulation.

Technical & Platform Requirements

  • Benchmark Needs (WP5): Scaffolding metric (hints vs. answers), misconception catalogs (failure patterns for Session 2), and curricular coverage validation.
  • Platform Needs (WP2): Telemetry logging (timestamped prompt logs for WP7), vendor masking (neutral interface, no branding), and GDPR pseudonymity (unique IDs).
