Intervention Design & Co-Creation Workshop

Structuring AI literacy interventions across STEM disciplines, learning goals, experimental arms, and platform requirements.

Lead: IE-UL (Instituto de Educação, ULisboa)  |  Co-Lead: UNIC · Core Partners: UU, UJA, HU, IPN, ICSE

Three-Arm Cluster-Randomised Design

  • GROUP A: AI use in class with instructional support (AI + instruction)
  • GROUP B: AI use in class without instructional support (AI, no instruction)
  • GROUP C (BASELINE CONTROL): No AI use in class (baseline) (No AI Integration)

The 3-Sessions Intervention

  • 1. Foundational AI Interaction: Students practice prompt formulation, engage with AI explanations, and solve curriculum-aligned tasks. Aim: to establish AI as a learning tool and build prompt engineering competence.
  • 2. Critical AI Literacy: Students work with flawed AI outputs, evaluate the reliability of claims using STEM knowledge, and learn to apply verification strategies. Aim: foster critical AI use and epistemic Awareness.
  • 3. Authentic Inquiry: Students tackle real-world STEM problems (e.g., plastic consumption), synthesising AI-generated and external information to construct arguments under uncertainty. Aim: To develop interdisciplinary reasoning and scientific literacy.
